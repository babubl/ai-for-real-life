About & FAQ

A free, honest place to start with AI.

People keep asking the same questions: how do I learn AI, where do I start, is there a good paid course? There's plenty of material online, but most of it is either hype, too technical, or hours of video without one useful result. This site is the answer we wished existed.

How it's made

  • Free, with no catch. No ads, no sign-up, no paid upsell, no affiliate links. Tools are mentioned because they're useful, not because anyone paid.
  • Benchmarked against real curricula. The content is mapped topic by topic to Harvard, University of Helsinki, Google, Microsoft, Anthropic, CBSE and UNESCO courses and frameworks. See the syllabus.
  • Learn by doing. Every idea comes with something to try in minutes, on a real task from your own life.
  • Honest about cost and limits. Where a step needs a paid plan, it says so, and offers a free alternative.
  • Made for India. Indian examples, rupee costs, Indian-language tips and Indian safety resources. A Tamil version is next.
  • Kept current. Each page shows when it was last reviewed. AI changes monthly, and so does this site.

Frequently asked questions

Is it really free?

Yes. Everything on this site is free to read and use. Most AI tools in the course have free versions that are enough to learn. A few steps, mainly some of the automated agents in Weeks 9 to 11, work best with a paid plan of a third-party tool; those are clearly labelled, each with a free alternative.

Where should I start?

Take the 2-minute quiz. It gives you a route based on who you are and how much time you have. If you'd rather not, do the 15-minute first win, then Week 1.

Do I need to understand the theory first?

No. Many people learn better by using AI for a few weeks, then reading How AI works once they're curious. If you prefer to understand first, start there. Both orders work.

Do I get a certificate?

Not from this site. You get a shareable progress card when you work through the course. If you need a certificate for a job or college, see the free and low-cost certificates below.

Do I need a laptop?

No for most of it. Weeks 1 to 7 and the How AI works lessons work on a phone. Weeks 8 to 12 (agents and building a website) are much easier on a laptop or desktop.

Do I need to know coding or English well?

No coding at all. Simple English is enough, and the AI assistants themselves work in Tamil, Hindi and other Indian languages. See AI in Indian languages.

How much time does it take?

Choose your pace: about 1 hour a week (around 30 weeks), 2 to 3 hours (around 18 weeks), or 4 to 5 hours (12 weeks). The course page lets you pick a schedule pattern that fits your mornings, evenings or weekends.

Will AI take my job?

So far, AI changes tasks more than it removes whole jobs, and the long-term effect is genuinely uncertain. Learning to use AI in your own field is the most practical step. Read AI and your job.

Is it safe to use AI?

Yes, with sensible habits: don't paste passwords, ID or bank details, check important facts, and know how AI scams work. The family safety guide covers it in ten minutes.

Can I share this with my class, team or family?

Please do. Share the link freely. Teachers and trainers are welcome to use it in sessions.

What AI tools cost

Every major assistant has a free version. Paid plans give more usage, newer models and extra features. In India, they typically range from a few hundred to around two thousand rupees a month, and prices and offers change often. Check the app itself for current Indian pricing.

  • Don't pay until you've hit a free limit twice on real work.
  • Pay for one, not five. The big assistants overlap heavily.
  • Watch for offers: for example, OpenAI offered ChatGPT Go free for a year to users in India from November 2025. Offers like this end, so check your plan and renewal date.
  • Automation tools such as Zapier usually need a paid plan for multi-step automations. The course labels each step.

Privacy

This site has no accounts, no cookies, no ads and no analytics or tracking. Your quiz answers, ticked missions and lesson progress are saved only in your own browser (local storage) on your own device. Nothing is sent to us. Clearing your browser data erases it.

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which means your browser contacts Google's servers to fetch them. External links take you to other sites with their own privacy policies.
