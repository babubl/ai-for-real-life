Start today

Your first useful result, in 15 minutes.

Most people give up on AI courses after hours of videos without one useful result. So start the other way round. Open ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini on your phone (all have free versions), start the timer, and follow three steps.

15:00

  1. Explain something confusing

    5 minutes

    Photograph an electricity bill, a school circular or an insurance letter. Attach it and paste:

    Explain this in simple words. What is it, what are the important numbers or dates, and do I need to do anything?

    For a medicine label, ask the same, then confirm with your doctor or pharmacist.

  2. Talk to it

    5 minutes

    Tap the microphone or voice button and speak naturally: "I want to plan a weekend trip near my city for a family of four on a small budget. Ask me questions first." Answer out loud, like a conversation.

  3. Make it yours

    5 minutes

    Pick a real task from this week:

    Here's something I need to sort out this week: [describe it]. Ask me up to 5 questions, one at a time, then give me a simple step-by-step plan.

Do I need a paid course?

To get started, no.

The basics are free, and the best free material comes from the companies building AI and from universities. It's also more current: AI tools change every few months, and a course recorded last year may show tools that have already changed. And watching videos isn't the same as using AI.

Better free options

  • This site: How AI works and the 12-week course
  • Elements of AI, University of Helsinki, with a free certificate
  • Anthropic Academy and OpenAI Academy
  • DeepLearning.AI "AI for Everyone", free to audit
  • SWAYAM and NPTEL for India-recognised certificates

When paying is worth it

  • You need a recognised certificate for a job or promotion
  • You want technical skills: Python, machine learning, data science
  • You need a live cohort and a mentor who reviews your work
  • You've done the free basics and know exactly what's missing

Warning signs

  • Income promises: "earn ₹1 lakh a month with ChatGPT"
  • "Secret prompts" or "1,000-prompt packs"
  • Countdown timers and a sale that never ends
  • No last-updated date, or older than six months
  • All videos, no exercises or projects

Before buying any course: check when it was last updated, watch the free preview, read the 3-star reviews, and check the refund policy. Free certificates you can earn

Family AI safety

AI makes scams more convincing. Ten minutes here protects your family.

  • The "emergency" callA cloned voice of a relative asking for money. Agree a family code word; hang up and call back.
  • Celebrity investment videosFamous faces promoting apps with guaranteed returns are usually deepfakes.
  • "AI trading bots"Fixed profits from a bot is a scam. Check SEBI registration of any adviser.
  • Never pasteOTPs, passwords, Aadhaar, PAN, card or bank details into any chatbot.
  • Fake AI appsInstall only official apps. Check the publisher: OpenAI, Anthropic, Google.
  • Children and AIIt can be wrong and it isn't a friend. Use it together at first.
  • Check before forwardingShocking photo or video? Look for odd details and check a news site.
  • Privacy settingsTurn off training on your chats and review what the assistant remembers.
